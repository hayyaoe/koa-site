Support

How can we help?

Koa is an offline EPUB reader for iPhone and iPad running iOS 17 or later.

Email koa@hayyaoe.dev with the app version, your device model, and what happened. A screenshot helps if the issue is visual.

Common questions

How do I add a book?

Open the Library tab, tap the + button, and choose an EPUB file from Files. Koa copies the book into its local library; the original file is left where it is.

Does Koa upload my books or reading data?

No. Koa reads and stores everything locally on your device. There is no account, no sync service, and no analytics. See the privacy policy for details.

My widget says there is no book or no activity.

Open Koa once and read a page so the shared widget data is refreshed, then wait a moment for the widget timeline to update. Widgets read the same local library through an Apple App Group; they need at least one imported book to show reading progress.

Can I sync between devices?

Not currently. Koa is deliberately local-first, so there is no account or cloud sync. Your device backups are handled by iOS, not by a Koa server.

Which formats are supported?

EPUB is the reading format. Koa does not currently open PDF, MOBI, or CBZ files.

How do I restore my purchase?

Koa is a paid app with no in-app purchases or subscription. Apple ties the App Store purchase to your Apple ID, so you can download it again from the App Store using the same account.

How do I delete a book or my data?

In the library, use the book's context menu to remove it. Removing a book deletes its local record and file. Deleting the app removes Koa's local data, subject to any device backups you have enabled.

Still stuck?

Email koa@hayyaoe.dev. Please include the app version from the About card on the Typography tab, your iOS version, and the steps that led to the problem.
