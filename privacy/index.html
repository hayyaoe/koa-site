Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Koa is designed to work without an account and without collecting your data. This policy explains what the app stores, where it goes, and the choices you have.

The short version

  • Koa does not collect, transmit, or sell personal data.
  • There is no account, analytics SDK, advertising SDK, or third-party tracking code.
  • Your library, reading progress, bookmarks, and statistics are stored on your device.
  • The app does not need a network connection to read.

What Koa stores on your device

When you import an EPUB, Koa stores the book file and a local record containing things like the title, author, cover, reading position, bookmarks, and reading statistics. This information lives in the app's local storage on your device.

Widgets

Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets read the same local data through an Apple App Group so they can show your current book, streak, heatmap, or a bookmarked quote. The data stays on your device; widgets do not send it anywhere.

Network access

Koa does not require network access to read. The app does not upload your library, reading history, or bookmarks to a server. Support and policy links open in your browser when you choose them.

Purchases

Koa is a paid app. Payment, receipts, and refunds are handled by Apple. Koa does not receive your payment details.

Deleting your data

Removing a book removes its local file and record from Koa. Deleting the app removes Koa's local data, subject to any device or iCloud backups you have enabled. Koa does not maintain a server-side copy that you would need to delete separately.

Children

Koa does not create accounts or knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. The books you import are your own content.

Changes to this policy

If Koa's data practices change, this page will be updated and the date above will change. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.

Contact
